# GapQuest mode: Late-Night Food Run

Read this when the student's request matches this mode (see the Modes table in SKILL.md). Commands run from the GapQuest folder. The Replies and asking-first rules in SKILL.md apply here too.

It's 1 am, the dining hall closed at midnight, and every map result says "Closes soon". The student wants three
options that are really open, that they can afford, and that fit how they eat, in one text.

## When to use

- "what's open rn"
- "starving, anything near Hayden library still open?"
- "cheap food that delivers to my dorm"
- "is the dining hall open"

Do not use it for meal plans, grocery lists or recipes.

## Steps

1. **Know the basics.** Use what Cubbie remembers about the student: campus and where they usually are (dorm or
   apartment), diet (vegetarian, halal, allergies), usual budget per meal, and whether they walk, bike, drive or
   want delivery. If any of these is missing, ask only for what this search needs, one question at a time, with a
   default ("Budget under $15?"), and remember the answer.

2. **Use the student's local time.** Take the current time and day in the student's time zone, not the computer's.
   Something "open" must stay open at least 30 minutes past now (longer if they will walk far), so they don't arrive
   to locked doors.

3. **Check campus dining first.** Search the web for the student's school's dining hours page for today, and read it.
   Note any dining hall, market or campus cafe that is open now and the last time to get in. Campus options are
   often the cheapest, and meal swipes may cover them.

4. **Search nearby.** Search the web for places open now near the student's location: fast food, diners, pizza,
   convenience stores with hot food, and delivery options that reach them. For each candidate, read its own page or
   listing to confirm today's hours; skip any whose hours you can't confirm. Check that the menu has something that
   fits the diet and a typical price within budget.

5. **Pick three.** Rank by: really open (with margin), fits diet, fits budget, closest or fastest delivery. Mix in a
   campus option when one is open.

6. **Reply** (see Replies).

7. **If they want to order.** Ask which one. Open the ordering page in Cubbie's browser, build the cart with what they
   ask for, then hand the browser to the student to sign in and pay. Never enter payment details and never place
   the order yourself.

8. **Remember what they picked** so next time their favorite comes first if it's open.

## Replies

Short, plain text, no Markdown. Lead with the best option. One line per place:

"3 open near you right now:
1. Taco spot on University Dr: open till 3 am, 6 min walk, burrito ~$9 (veg)
2. Campus market: till 1:30 am, meal swipe works
3. Pizza place: delivers till 2 am, ~35 min, slice combo $8"

Put any link on its own line after the list. If it's very late, add one line like "Walk with a friend or grab
delivery" only when the walk is long; don't lecture.

## When something fails

- Nothing is confirmed open: say so, give the earliest place that opens next and a 24-hour convenience store if one
  exists, and offer to set a reminder for when the dining hall opens.
- The student's location is unknown: ask for their campus or the nearest landmark, and remember it.
- Hours conflict between sources: trust the place's own page, and say "hours may vary, call ahead" for that one.
